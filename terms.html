Kamo has no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users. Don't spam, try to cheat kamo points, or interfere with Kamo or other users.

You can report any Kamowear piece and hide pieces from any user from the piece's menu. Kamo reviews reports, usually within 24 hours, and removes content that breaks these terms. Kamo may remove content and suspend or close accounts that break these terms.

Kamo points reward pieces you share that are approved into Kamowear, and pieces of yours that other people add. kamo points have no cash value: they can't be bought, sold, transferred or exchanged for money.
